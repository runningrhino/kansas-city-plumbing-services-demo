Our Plumbing Services

We provide comprehensive plumbing services throughout the Kansas City metro area. From emergency repairs to installations, our licensed plumbers are ready to help.

🚨

Emergency Plumber Kansas City MO

24/7 emergency plumbing service. Burst pipes, flooding, sewage backups, gas leaks — we respond fast to keep your home and family safe. Don't wait until a small problem becomes a big one.

🚿

Drain Cleaning Services

Clogged drains are one of the most common plumbing problems. We use professional-grade equipment to clear stubborn clogs and restore full drainage. Slow drains, complete blockages, and sewer line cleaning — we handle them all.

🔥

Water Heater Repair & Installation

We take care of water heater installations and repairs immediately. Our technicians assess whether you need repair or replacement, then give you a clear recommendation. Tank and tankless systems — we service them all.

🏠

Residential Plumbing

Clogged drains in your home can cause havoc. We take special measures to ensure your home is back to normal fast. Services include: leak repair, main sewer line repair, frozen pipes, drain cleaning, toilet repair, water heater repair, washing machine repair, faucet repair, bathroom installations, clog repairs, water pressure issues, dishwasher repair, garbage disposal repairs, and downspout repairs.

🏢

Commercial Plumbing

Businesses need reliable plumbing too. We provide toilet drains, drain and sewer line repair, sink drains, excavation, grease trap cleaning, broken pipes, trenchless repairs, water jetting, camera line inspection, backflow services, disposal services, faucet services, flushometer services, sump pump services, water heater services, and more.

🔧

General Plumbing Repair

Housing equipment often doesn't need to be replaced — just mended. Our repairs include water leaks, toilet bowls, sinks, shower and bath tubs, broken pipes, frozen pipes, and water lines, amongst many others.
